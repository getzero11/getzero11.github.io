01

About

Biotechnology taught me to ask better questions. Research taught me to defend the answers. Software lets me repeat them.

My path ran from biotechnology and molecular biology research, to postdoctoral molecular bacteriology and neuroscience through healthcare market research, and into building AI agentic systems. I have published 100+ research reports across medical devices, pharmaceuticals, diagnostics and digital health.

Before building AI products, I worked across scientific research, biotechnology R&D, technology commercialisation, teaching and healthcare consulting. That mix gives me a practical view of how research becomes a product: from evidence and validation to market context, delivery and collaboration.

AI Automation Stack

A self-contained agent platform that runs anywhere: n8n for orchestration, Claude Code with an MCP control layer, a FastAPI agent API, local Qwen models through Ollama, and Supabase for durable state. Packaged for Docker and Hugging Face Spaces.

OutcomeOne stack for building and running agent workflows without stitching five vendors together.
